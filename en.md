## Leadership & Working Principles

**Standards create freedom.** Clear goals, responsibilities and technical guardrails reduce complexity and enable autonomous work.

**Technology must serve a purpose.** New technology matters when it solves user problems, simplifies processes or measurably improves products and services.

**Product development requires market understanding.** User feedback, market and competitor analysis, technical feasibility, commercial viability and operability belong together.

**Change must be usable.** Transformation becomes sustainable when experts and users are involved and the practical benefit is visible.
